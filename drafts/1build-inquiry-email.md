Hi 1build team,

I'm reaching out from **Ungouge.ai**, a new platform that helps homeowners verify contractor quotes with data-driven analysis.

**What we do:**  
Homeowners submit contractor quotes (roofing, HVAC, kitchens, etc.), and we analyze them against market data to help them understand if the pricing is fair. We're explicitly **not** a lead generation service — we never sell user data, never refer contractors, and we don't take commissions. Our only revenue is a $19.99 analysis fee paid by the homeowner.

**Why we're reaching out:**  
Your API is exactly what we need to provide accurate, localized cost benchmarking across all US markets (we're launching nationally). Real-time pricing data from major suppliers + regional variations + labor rates = the foundation of trustworthy analysis.

**Our positioning:**  
We see this as **pro-good-contractor**, not anti-contractor. Transparent pricing helps reputable contractors compete fairly against bad actors. When homeowners understand what's reasonable, they're more confident hiring contractors who price honestly — and they can spot the outliers who are trying to overcharge.

**Questions:**
1. What does API access look like? (Pricing tiers, rate limits, coverage areas)
2. Do you have sample data or documentation we could review?
3. Any restrictions on use case (homeowner education vs contractor tools)?

Would love to explore how we can work together to bring transparency to the home improvement market.
