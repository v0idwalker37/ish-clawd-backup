### Estimated Quotes v7 — Major Rearchitecture (Deployed rev 00065)
Jason pointed out the core problem: rating AI-invented line item prices as fair/gouging is BS. Redesigned the entire estimated quote experience:

**New approach:**
- **Total-level analysis only** — "Is $24,636 fair for a bathroom remodel in Vermont?" (uses Gemini 2.5 Pro + Search Grounding)
- **Educational cost ranges** — "Tile installation typically costs $3,000–$5,500 in your area" (independent market data, NOT summing to total)
- **No per-item ratings** — no gauges, no fair/gouging assessments on items we don't know the price of
- **Clear CTA** — "Want per-item analysis? Ask contractor for itemized breakdown"
