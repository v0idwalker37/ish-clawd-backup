## Implementation Options

### Option A: Manual Curation (5 min/day)
- I scan sources during 1-4 AM deep work
- Curate top 3-5 items
- Include in 6 AM briefing
- **Pros:** High quality, relevant
- **Cons:** Manual work daily

### Option B: Automated Aggregation
- RSS feeds from HN, TechCrunch, Product Hunt
- Keyword filter ("AI", "LLM", "agent", "launch")
- Auto-summarize top 5
- **Pros:** Fully automated
- **Cons:** Lower signal-to-noise

### Option C: Weekly Digest (Lighter)
- Deeper summary once per week (Monday AM)
- More analysis, less daily noise
- Good for strategic thinking
- **Pros:** Less overwhelm, more insight
- **Cons:** Miss fast-moving news
