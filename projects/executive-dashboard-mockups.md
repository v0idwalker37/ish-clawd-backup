## Recommendations

### For Jason's Use Case

**Best fit: Concept 4 (Morning Briefing)**
- Matches his current workflow (already gets morning briefings)
- Integrates "amazing AI things" naturally
- Conversational, not corporate
- Action-oriented for his 20 hrs/week commitment
- Can be delivered to Telegram at 6 AM

**Alternative: Concept 1 (Single-Pane)**
- If he wants a persistent dashboard (not just morning)
- Quick glance during the day
- Good for checking progress without digging into files

### Implementation Options

**Option A: Enhanced Morning Briefing (Easiest)**
- Expand existing 6 AM cron job
- Add sections from Concept 4
- Deliver to Telegram as formatted message
- No new infrastructure needed

**Option B: Web Dashboard (More Complex)**
- Simple HTML page served locally
- Auto-refreshes from project data
- Accessible from phone/Mac
- Requires: Small web server, data aggregation scripts

**Option C: PDF Daily Report (Middle Ground)**
- Generate PDF each morning
- Email or save to known location
- Professional looking
- Good for sharing/archiving
