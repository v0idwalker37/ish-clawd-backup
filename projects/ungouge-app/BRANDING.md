# Ungouge.ai Branding Guide

**Version 1.0** | Last Updated: January 2025

## Brand Overview

**Mission:** Empower homeowners to verify contractor quotes with data—never with lead-gen tricks.

**Positioning:** The honest data partner that protects homeowners from overpriced quotes. We're the contractor pricing watchdog that puts consumers first.

**Core Promise:** Transparent pricing intelligence for $19.99. No hidden agendas. No selling your data. Ever.

## 1. Logo Concepts

### Concept A: The Shield Guardian (Primary Recommendation)

**Visual Description:**
A modern shield icon with a price tag or dollar sign integrated into the center. The shield is formed by two upward-curving geometric shapes that create negative space in the middle. The wordmark "Ungouge" sits beside or below in a bold, confident sans-serif, with ".ai" in a lighter weight.

**Detailed Elements:**
- Shield outline: 3px stroke weight, rounded corners (4px radius)
- Central element: Stylized "$" or price tag icon, 60% size of shield height
- Shield proportions: 1:1.15 ratio (slightly taller than wide)
- Negative space creates subtle upward arrow suggesting "fair price" direction

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: `#0F4C81` (Trust Blue) for shield outline
- Accent: `#00B894` (Fair Green) for center element
- Text: `#1A1D29` (Charcoal) for "Ungouge"
- Secondary text: `#6B7280` (Slate Gray) for ".ai"

**Usage:**
- **Large format (>200px):** Full shield + wordmark horizontal lockup
- **Small format (<200px):** Shield icon only, or stacked wordmark below
- **Minimum size:** 24px height for icon-only version
- **Clear space:** 0.5x shield height on all sides

**Reasoning:**
The shield is universally recognized as protection and trust. By integrating pricing symbolism, we immediately communicate "price protection." The modern, geometric approach avoids looking like insurance/security companies while maintaining authority. The upward negative space subtly suggests "getting a fair deal" without being heavy-handed.

---

### Concept B: The Price Gauge

**Visual Description:**
A semicircular gauge/meter inspired by speedometers and measurement tools. The needle points to the "fair" zone (left third of gauge, green). The arc is divided into three color zones: fair, high, overprice. The wordmark "Ungouge" appears below with ".ai" integrated as part of the gauge's base.

**Detailed Elements:**
- Gauge arc: 180° semicircle, 6px stroke weight
- Three zones: 60° fair (green), 60° caution (amber), 60° gouge (red)
- Needle: Sharp triangle pointer at -45° angle (pointing to fair zone)
- Base integration: ".ai" sits in negative space at gauge base
- Arc endpoints have subtle glow/highlight effect

**Color Palette:**
- Fair zone: `#10B981` (Success Green)
- Caution zone: `#F59E0B` (Warning Amber)
- Gouge zone: `#EF4444` (Alert Red)
- Needle: `#1F2937` (Near Black)
- Wordmark: `#111827` (Charcoal Black)
- ".ai" accent: `#6366F1` (Tech Indigo)

**Usage:**
- **Large format:** Full gauge with wordmark, all color zones visible
- **Small format:** Simplified gauge (single color arc) with needle
- **Minimum size:** 32px for legibility of zones
- **Animation potential:** Needle can "sweep" to position on loading states

**Reasoning:**
The gauge is instantly recognizable as a measurement tool, reinforcing data-driven analysis. The visual metaphor of "where does your quote land?" is powerful and educational. The three-zone color system becomes a semantic pattern used throughout the product. This concept is highly distinctive in the home services space.

---

### Concept C: Clean Wordmark with Data Accent (Minimal Recommendation)

**Visual Description:**
A bold, geometric wordmark "UNGOUGE" with a unique ligature or accent that suggests data/analysis. The "UN" prefix could be underlined or the "U" could incorporate a subtle upward-trending line chart. ".ai" appears in a contrasting color as a raised superscript or inline with lighter weight.

**Detailed Elements:**
- Wordmark: Custom-drawn or modified Neue Haas Grotesk Display Bold
- "UN" prefix: Optional underline treatment (2px, 8px length)
- Data accent: 3-bar micro chart icon integrated into "U" or standalone
- ".ai" treatment: 60% size of main wordmark, elevated 20% baseline
- Tracking: -2% for tight, confident spacing

**Color Palette:**
- Primary wordmark: `#0A0E27` (Deep Navy)
- ".ai" accent: `#3B82F6` (Data Blue)
- Optional underline: `#10B981` (Success Green)
- Background contrast version: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)

**Usage:**
- **All formats:** Horizontal lockup, scalable from 16px to billboard size
- **Small format advantage:** Most legible at tiny sizes (favicons, app icons)
- **Minimum size:** 48px width for full detail, 24px for simplified version
- **Monochrome fallback:** Excellent - works in single color

**Reasoning:**
When you're confident in your value proposition, sometimes less is more. This approach emphasizes the name "Ungouge" itself—already a strong, memorable, action-oriented brand name. Perfect for digital-first contexts. The data accent subtly reinforces the analytical nature without requiring explanation. This works exceptionally well for YouTube thumbnails (UnGouge Digest) where text legibility is critical.

## 3. Typography

### Heading Font: **Inter Display (Variable)**

**Rationale:**
- Modern, geometric, confident
- Excellent legibility at all sizes
- Variable font allows dynamic weight control (400-900)
- Tech-forward but approachable
- Free and open-source (no licensing issues)

**Usage:**
- **H1:** 48px / 56px line height / 700 weight / -1% tracking
- **H2:** 36px / 44px line height / 700 weight / -0.5% tracking
- **H3:** 28px / 36px line height / 600 weight / 0% tracking
- **H4:** 24px / 32px line height / 600 weight / 0% tracking
- **H5:** 20px / 28px line height / 500 weight / 0% tracking

**Fallback Stack:**
```css
font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', 'Roboto', sans-serif;
```

---

### Body Font: **Inter (Regular)**

**Rationale:**
Using the same font family for body creates cohesion while reducing page weight. Inter was designed for screen legibility and performs exceptionally well in long-form content.

**Usage:**
- **Body Large:** 18px / 28px line height / 400 weight / 0% tracking
- **Body Regular:** 16px / 24px line height / 400 weight / 0% tracking
- **Body Small:** 14px / 20px line height / 400 weight / 0% tracking
- **Caption:** 12px / 16px line height / 400 weight / 0.5% tracking

---

### Monospace Font: **JetBrains Mono** (for data/numbers)

**Rationale:**
- Excellent for displaying pricing data, report details
- High legibility for numbers and currency
- Modern monospace that doesn't look "code-y"
- Clear distinction between digits (0 vs O)

**Usage:**
- Price displays: 24-32px / 600 weight
- Data tables: 14px / 400 weight
- Report numbers: 16px / 500 weight

**Fallback Stack:**
```css
font-family: 'JetBrains Mono', 'SF Mono', Monaco, 'Courier New', monospace;
```

---

### Typography Guidelines

**Rules:**
1. **Never use more than 3 font weights per page** (usually 400, 600, 700)
2. **Maintain 1.5x minimum line height** for body copy (accessibility)
3. **Use sentence case for most UI** (buttons, labels, navigation)
4. **Use title case for marketing headlines** (landing page, ads)
5. **Tabular numbers for all pricing** (use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`)

**Special Treatments:**
- **Pricing displays:** Always JetBrains Mono, minimum 20px, 600 weight
- **"Ungouge" brand name:** Always capitalize as shown, never all-caps
- **".ai" suffix:** Always lowercase, slightly smaller and lighter weight
- **CTAs:** 600 weight, 0.5px letter spacing for emphasis

## 4. Brand Voice Guide

### Tone: **Honest, Protective, Empowering**

You're the friend who knows the industry and isn't afraid to tell homeowners the truth. You're confident without being arrogant. You're data-driven without being robotic. You're protective without being alarmist.

**Voice Attributes:**
- **Transparent:** Never hide behind jargon
- **Confident:** Backed by data, not opinions
- **Conversational:** Like talking to a knowledgeable neighbor
- **Anti-BS:** Call out industry practices that hurt consumers
- **Empowering:** You give homeowners the tools to make smart decisions

---

### Words to Use

**Positive/Empowering:**
- Verify, confirm, check, compare
- Fair, reasonable, typical, average
- Protect, empower, informed, confident
- Data-driven, transparent, honest
- Value, savings, smart decision

**Data/Analysis:**
- Based on local data, our analysis shows, market rate
- Comparable projects, regional pricing, cost breakdown
- Industry standards, typical range, benchmark

**Problem/Solution:**
- Overpriced, inflated, above market rate
- Peace of mind, second opinion, verification
- Know before you pay, price intelligence

---

### Words to AVOID

**Lead-Gen Language (NEVER USE):**
- ❌ "Get matched with contractors"
- ❌ "Connect you with pros"
- ❌ "Request quotes from our network"
- ❌ "Top-rated contractors in your area"
- ❌ "Free consultation" (implies we sell leads)

**Scare Tactics:**
- ❌ "You're being ripped off!" (too aggressive)
- ❌ "Contractors are lying to you" (creates distrust)
- ❌ "Don't get scammed!" (alarmist)

**Vague Marketing Fluff:**
- ❌ "Industry-leading solutions"
- ❌ "Revolutionary platform"
- ❌ "Next-generation technology"
- ❌ "Cutting-edge innovation"

**Judgmental/Negative:**
- ❌ "Stupid homeowner mistakes"
- ❌ "Obviously overpriced"
- ❌ "Any idiot can see..."

---

### Example Copy

#### Landing Page Hero
**Good:**
> "Get a data-backed second opinion on any contractor quote. $19.99. No lead gen. No BS."

**Why it works:** Clear value prop, transparent pricing, anti-lead-gen stance up front.

**Bad:**
> "Connect with top-rated contractors and get competitive quotes for your home project!"

**Why it fails:** Sounds like every lead-gen site. No differentiation.

---

#### Price Assessment (Fair)
**Good:**
> "✓ Fair Price
> 
> This quote aligns with local market rates for comparable projects. Our analysis shows this is within the typical range for your area based on [X] similar projects."

**Why it works:** Specific, data-backed, reassuring without overselling.

---

#### Price Assessment (Gouging)
**Good:**
> "⚠ Above Market Rate
> 
> This quote is 34% higher than the average for comparable projects in your area. Based on local data, a fair price range would be $X,XXX - $X,XXX. Consider getting additional quotes."

**Why it works:** Specific percentage, actionable guidance, provides alternative range.

**Bad:**
> "SCAM ALERT! This contractor is trying to rip you off! Report them immediately!"

**Why it fails:** Alarmist, accusatory, unhelpful. Could be liability issue.

---

#### Anti-Lead-Gen Messaging
**Use prominently on site/reports:**

> "**We Never Sell Your Data**
> 
> Unlike other quote comparison sites, we don't sell your information to contractors. We're not a lead generation service. You pay us $19.99, we give you data. That's it. No hidden agendas."

**Why it works:** Direct, transparent, differentiates from competitors.

---

#### YouTube Channel (UnGouge Digest)

**Intro Script Template:**

**Outro Script Template:**

## 5. Visual Style

### Photography

**Primary Style: Data Over Lifestyle**

Don't use generic stock photos of smiling contractors and perfect homes. You're a data company, not a contractor marketplace.

**Do Use:**
- Screenshots of actual reports with data highlighted
- Close-ups of quote documents (anonymized)
- Data visualization screenshots
- Before/after pricing comparisons
- Real homeowner testimonials with photo permission

**Don't Use:**
- Stock photos of contractors in hard hats pointing at things
- Overly staged "happy homeowner" photos
- Generic construction imagery
- Handshake photos

**Treatment:**
- Slight desaturation (10-15%) for authenticity
- Add subtle data overlays when relevant (price callouts, arrows)
- Use screenshots with clean borders and drop shadows for depth
- Real over perfect

---

### Icons

**Style: Outlined, Geometric, Consistent Stroke**

**Specifications:**
- 2px stroke weight
- 24x24px base grid
- Rounded line caps (4px radius)
- Minimal internal details
- Heroicons or Lucide icon sets recommended

**Key Icons:**
- Shield (protection/verification)
- Gauge/meter (price assessment)
- Check circle (fair price)
- Alert triangle (high price)
- X circle (gouging price)
- Document (quote/report)
- Bar chart (data/analysis)
- Eye (review/verify)
- Calculator (pricing tool)
- Lock (data security)

**Color Treatment:**
- Default: `#6B7280` (Slate Gray)
- Interactive: `#0F4C81` (Trust Blue)
- Semantic: Use green/amber/red based on context

---

### Data Visualization

**Style: Clean, Colorful, Immediately Readable**

**Chart Types:**
1. **Price Gauge (Primary):** Semicircular gauge showing quote position
2. **Range Bars:** Show quote vs. market range
3. **Comparison Tables:** Your quote vs. local average
4. **Trend Lines:** Historical pricing data for project type
5. **Distribution Curves:** Where quotes typically fall

**Design Principles:**
- **Large labels:** 14px minimum for all text
- **High contrast:** Always pass WCAG AA
- **Color coding:** Green/amber/red semantic system
- **Tooltips:** Explain data points on hover
- **Mobile-first:** All charts must work on small screens

**Library Recommendation:**
- Recharts (React) or Chart.js (vanilla JS)
- Custom color palette (use brand colors)
- Disable animations for printing/PDFs

---

### Overall Feel

**Modern Data Platform, Not Generic Home Services Site**

Think: Stripe, Plaid, Bloomberg Terminal meets Consumer Reports.

**Design Patterns:**
- **Cards with subtle shadows** (0-2px, 10% opacity)
- **Generous white space** (24-32px padding on cards)
- **Bold section headers** (Inter Display, 700 weight)
- **Inline data highlights** (colored backgrounds, monospace font)
- **Scannable reports** (clear hierarchy, visual anchors)

**Layout:**
- Wide, centered containers (max-width: 1200px)
- Sidebar navigation for reports (sticky on desktop)
- Mobile: single column, collapsible sections
- Print-friendly report layouts (hidden nav, expanded sections)

**Micro-interactions:**
- Button hover: slight lift + shadow increase
- Card hover: border color change
- Gauge animation: needle sweep on load (1s duration)
- Success states: green checkmark animation
- Loading states: skeleton screens (no spinners)

## 6. Favicon & Social Assets

### Favicon (16x16 / 32x32)

**Recommendation: Shield Icon (Simplified)**

At 16x16, details disappear. Use an ultra-simplified shield:
- Solid fill shield shape (no stroke)
- Single color: `#0F4C81` (Trust Blue)
- No internal details
- Slightly rounded corners for softness

**32x32 Version:**
- Can add dollar sign or checkmark in center
- Use two-color version (blue shield + green center)

**Technical:**
- Provide `.ico` file with multiple sizes embedded (16, 32, 48)
- Provide `apple-touch-icon.png` (180x180) with 20px padding
- Use SVG for `manifest.json` (maskable icon support)

**Alternative (Wordmark):**
If shield doesn't read at small size, use bold "U" lettermark:
- Custom-drawn "U" with data accent line
- Solid color on transparent background
- Higher contrast for visibility
