## Linking Rules

### Every Cost Breakdown Post Should Link To:
1. "How to Tell If Your Contractor Quote Is Too High" (general evaluation)
2. "How to Read a Contractor Quote" (understanding quotes)
3. UnGouge quote verification CTA
4. 1-2 related cost breakdown posts (same category: kitchen → bathroom, roof → siding, etc.)

### Every Educational Post Should Link To:
1. 2-3 relevant cost breakdown posts (examples)
2. 1-2 related educational posts
3. UnGouge quote verification CTA

### CTA Link Format
Standard at end of each post:
```
## Get Your Quote Verified

Not sure if your contractor quote is fair? [Submit your quote to UnGouge](/analyze) for a detailed, data-backed analysis in 24 hours. We'll tell you if you're getting a fair price—or if you should negotiate.
```
