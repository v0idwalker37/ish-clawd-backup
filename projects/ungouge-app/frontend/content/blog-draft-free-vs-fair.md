## The Lead Gen Trap

Most "free" contractor quote tools aren't free. They're lead generation funnels.

Here's how they work:

1. You upload your contractor's quote
2. Their AI does a surface-level comparison
3. They recommend you "get more quotes" from their network
4. Your information — name, address, project details, budget — gets sold to 3-5 contractors
5. Those contractors paid $30-100 each for your contact info
6. Your phone starts ringing

That "free" tool just made $90-500 from your data. And the contractors calling you? They built that lead cost into their quote. You're paying for it whether you realize it or not.

## How to Spot a Lead Gen Tool

Red flags that a "contractor quote tool" is really a lead funnel:

- ✅ It's free (someone's paying — it's the contractors, who pass costs to you)
- ✅ It asks for your phone number or address
- ✅ It "recommends" contractors from "their network"
- ✅ It's owned by a contractor matching service
- ✅ No clear explanation of how they make money

## What Independent Analysis Looks Like

- 🔒 Never sells your data to anyone
- 🔒 Compares your quote against independent market data, not just other bids
