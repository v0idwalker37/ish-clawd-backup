# FAQ: Why isn't GougeAlert free like other quote tools?

## The short answer

Free quote tools make money by selling your information to contractors. We make money when you pay us $19.99. That's it.

## The longer answer

Most "free" contractor tools are **lead generation services** in disguise. Here's how they work:

1. You upload your project details and contact info
2. They provide a free analysis
3. They sell your lead to contractors for $50-150 each
4. Those contractors call, email, and text you

You're not the customer — you're the product being sold.

## What you get for $19.99

- Analysis against **independent market data** (not just bid-vs-bid comparison)
- Material cost breakdown based on current pricing
- Labor hour estimates for your project scope
- Regional pricing adjustments
- Clear verdict on whether your quote is fair
- **Your information never sold. Ever.**

## The math

If a free tool sells your info to 3 contractors at $50 each, they made $150. You're now fielding sales calls from contractors you didn't ask to hear from.

We made $19.99. From you. Because you're our customer.

## Our promise

GougeAlert will never:
- Sell your data to contractors
- Refer you to contractors for a fee
- Share your information with third parties
- Contact you with "partner offers"

We're not in the lead generation business. We're in the homeowner protection business.
