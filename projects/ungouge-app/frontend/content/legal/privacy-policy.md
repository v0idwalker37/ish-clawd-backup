# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 2026  
**Last Updated:** February 8, 2026

## The Short Version

We collect only what's necessary to analyze your contractor quote and deliver your report. We don't sell your data. We don't share it with contractors. We don't do lead generation. Period.

---

## Information We Collect

### When You Submit a Quote
- **Quote content:** The contractor quote you upload or paste (text, images, PDFs)
- **Email address:** To deliver your analysis report
- **Project location:** ZIP code or city/state (for regional price comparison)
- **Payment information:** Processed securely by Stripe; we don't store card numbers

### Automatically Collected
- **Basic analytics:** Page views, browser type, device type (via privacy-respecting analytics)
- **No tracking cookies:** We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies

---

## How We Use Your Information

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Quote content | Generate your fairness analysis | Deleted after 30 days |
| Email address | Deliver your report, account access | Until you unsubscribe |
| Project location | Regional price comparison | Deleted after 30 days |
| Payment info | Process your payment (via Stripe) | Handled by Stripe |

---

## What We DON'T Do

❌ **We don't sell your data** — to contractors, marketers, or anyone else  
❌ **We don't do lead generation** — no contractor referrals, no "matches"  
❌ **We don't share quotes** — your quote is analyzed privately  
❌ **We don't build profiles** — no behavioral tracking across the web  
❌ **We don't contact you unsolicited** — no sales calls, no spam

- Access limited to essential personnel only
