## Red Flags in Kitchen Remodel Quotes

Watch for these warning signs:

❌ **No itemized breakdown** — Every quote should show materials, labor, permits separately

❌ **"Allowances" for everything** — Vague allowances hide pricing until you're committed

❌ **No permit mention** — Kitchen remodels often require electrical/plumbing permits

❌ **50%+ deposit required** — Standard is 10-30% down, remainder in milestones

❌ **Way below market rates** — If it's 40% cheaper than everyone else, ask why

## How to Get the Best Price

1. **Get 3-5 quotes** — Same scope, same materials specified
2. **Time it right** — Winter (Jan-Feb) is slow season; contractors negotiate more
3. **Bundle projects** — Doing bathroom too? Package deals save money
4. **Keep the layout** — Moving plumbing/electric adds $5,000-15,000

## FAQs

### How long does a kitchen remodel take?
- Minor refresh: 2-4 weeks
- Mid-range renovation: 6-10 weeks
- Major remodel: 10-16 weeks

### Should I get a permit?
If you're doing electrical, plumbing, or structural work—yes. Always.

### Can I live in my house during the remodel?
Usually yes, but expect 1-2 weeks of no kitchen access. Plan for takeout.

### How much should I budget for overruns?
Add 10-20% contingency to any kitchen budget. Surprises happen.
