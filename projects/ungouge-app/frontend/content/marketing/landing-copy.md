## FAQ Section (10 Questions)

### 1. How does GougeAlert make money if you don't sell leads?
We charge homeowners a small fee for detailed analysis. Free tier available for basic comparison. No ads, no lead gen, no BS. You're the customer, not the product.

### 2. Is my quote data private?
Yes. We encrypt all uploads, never share your info with contractors, and only use anonymized data to improve our pricing benchmarks. Read our full [Privacy Policy](#).

### 3. What types of projects do you cover?
Roofing, kitchens, bathrooms, siding, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, windows, decks, basements, and more. If contractors bid on it, we probably have market data for it.

### 4. How accurate is your pricing data?
Our benchmarks are based on real project data, public records, and material costs. We account for regional variation, project complexity, and current market conditions. Not perfect, but way better than guessing.

### 5. What if my quote is higher than your benchmark?
Higher doesn't automatically mean bad. Premium materials, complex logistics, or contractor experience can justify higher prices. We flag suspicious items and let you decide.

### 6. Can I use GougeAlert to negotiate with my contractor?
Absolutely. That's the point. Showing data (not just "I think this is high") gives you leverage to ask questions and negotiate fairly.

### 7. Do contractors know about GougeAlert?
Some do. Good contractors don't mind because their pricing is fair. Shady ones... well, they're not fans. We're okay with that.

### 8. What if I don't have a quote yet?
Use our cost estimator tools to get ballpark ranges before you even meet with contractors. Helps you spot lowball bids (bad sign) and way-too-high bids (also bad).

### 9. Can I upload multiple quotes to compare?
Yes! Premium users can upload multiple quotes for the same project and see side-by-side comparison. Really useful for apples-to-apples analysis.

### 10. What if my contractor asks where I got pricing data?
Tell them! Informed homeowners make better clients. If a contractor is defensive about data-driven questions, that's a red flag in itself.

## Objection Handling Microcopy

### Objection 1: "Why should I pay for this?"
**Response:** Fair question. You're about to spend $10K, $50K, maybe $100K+ on a renovation. Would you rather guess if the price is fair, or know? Think of it as insurance against getting ripped off.

---

### Objection 2: "Can't I just Google pricing?"
**Response:** Sure! And you'll find ranges so wide they're useless. "$15K-$40K for a kitchen" doesn't tell you if your $32K quote is reasonable. We compare YOUR specific quote to actual market data.

---

### Objection 3: "My contractor will think I don't trust them"
**Response:** Good contractors welcome informed clients. If asking data-driven questions damages your contractor relationship, that's valuable information about who you're hiring.

---

### Objection 4: "What if you're wrong?"
**Response:** We show data and flag suspicious items; you make the final call. We're not guaranteeing pricing—we're giving you information to make better decisions. Still better than flying blind.
