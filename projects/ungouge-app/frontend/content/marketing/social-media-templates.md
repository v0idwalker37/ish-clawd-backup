```

1/ THE MYSTERY LINE ITEM

"Miscellaneous materials: $4,200"

What materials? For what?

This is a profit-padding catch-all.

❌ Red flag: Anything over $500 without itemization
✅ Ask: "Can you break this down into specific materials?"

---

2/ THE MATERIAL MARKUP MADNESS

"Quartz countertops: $8,500"

Contractor's cost: $3,000
Your cost: $8,500

That's 183% markup.

Standard markup: 20-35%
Excessive markup: 50%+

✅ Look up material costs yourself. Verify pricing.

---

3/ THE INVISIBLE LABOR

"Labor: $12,000"

No hourly rate. No hours specified.

Are they charging $50/hr or $150/hr?

You can't tell. That's the point.

✅ Ask for: hourly rate × estimated hours

---

4/ THE CONTINGENCY CUSHION

"Contingency: 20%"

Standard: 10-15%
Padding: 20%+

That extra 5-10% goes straight to profit margin.

✅ Negotiate down to 10-15% or require change orders for overages

---

5/ THE PERMIT PRICE INFLATION

"Permits & fees: $2,500"

Actual permit cost (public record): $400

Where's the other $2,100?

✅ Call your local building department. Verify permit costs yourself.

---

BOTTOM LINE:

If you can't verify a line item, question it.

Good contractors will explain.
Bad contractors will get defensive.

Want help analyzing your quote? 👉 gougealert.ai

No spam. No lead gen. Just honest data.
```
