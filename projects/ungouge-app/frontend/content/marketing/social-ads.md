## Testing Framework

### A/B Test Priority
1. **Messaging:** Anti-lead-gen emphasis vs Speed/convenience
2. **Visual Style:** Professional/data vs Casual/irreverent
3. **CTA:** "Learn More" vs "Upload Quote" vs "Get Started"
4. **Social Proof:** Testimonial vs Data-driven claims
5. **Tone:** Empowering vs Problem-focused

### Success Metrics
- CTR > 1.5% (Facebook feed)
- CPC < $3.00
- Landing page conversion > 15%
- Cost per quote uploaded < $20

### Creative Refresh Schedule
- New ad concepts every 2 weeks
- Pause ads with CTR < 0.8%
- Scale winners at 150% budget
