## General Campaign Settings Recommendations

### Negative Keywords
- contractor leads
- find a contractor
- contractor directory
- hire contractor
- contractor referral
- home advisor
- thumbtack alternative
- contractor matching service

### Audience Targeting
- In-market: Home Improvement Planning
- In-market: Home Renovation Services
- Custom Intent: Recently searched contractor-related terms
- Customer Match: Uploaded list (post-launch)

### Ad Schedule
- Increase bids during weekday evenings (6-9 PM) when homeowners review quotes
- Weekend afternoons (Sat-Sun 12-5 PM) for research

### Location Targeting
- Start with major metro areas where contractor pricing data is strongest
- Expand based on performance and data coverage

## A/B Testing Priorities

1. **Trust vs Speed:** "No Lead Gen" messaging vs "60-Second Analysis"
2. **Pain Point:** "Getting gouged" vs "Fair pricing"
3. **Tone:** Direct/irreverent vs Professional/data-focused
4. **CTA:** "Check Now" vs "Upload Quote" vs "Get Analysis"
