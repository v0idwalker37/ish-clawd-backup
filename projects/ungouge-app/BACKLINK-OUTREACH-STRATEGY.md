# Backlink Outreach Strategy

## Goal
Build high-quality backlinks to UnGouge.ai to improve domain authority and search rankings.

**Target:** 20-30 quality backlinks in first 60 days

## Link-Worthy Assets We Have

1. **State-specific cost data** (50 location pages)
2. **39 detailed cost guides** (blog posts with real pricing data)
3. **Unique positioning** (anti-lead-gen, data-driven quote analysis)
4. **Quote verification tool** (actual product, not just content)

## Target Link Sources

### Tier 1: High-Value, Reachable

#### Local Business Directories (DR 40-70)
- **Target sites:** Local Chamber of Commerce websites, Better Business Bureau, local business associations
- **Pitch:** "We're a Vermont-based contractor transparency tool. Would you list us in your member directory?"
- **Estimated success rate:** 60% (most welcome local businesses)

#### Home Improvement Forums/Communities
- **Target sites:** GardenWeb, Houzz forums, ContractorTalk, r/HomeImprovement sidebar
- **Pitch:** "We built a free resource (state cost guides) that your community might find useful."
- **Estimated success rate:** 30% (selective but relevant)

#### Local News "New Business" Features
- **Target sites:** VTDigger, Seven Days Vermont, local papers in major cities
- **Pitch:** "Vermont startup tackles contractor pricing transparency—would you cover it?"
- **Estimated success rate:** 20% (worth the effort for DR 50-60 links)

#### Home Services Review Sites
- **Target sites:** Porch, HomeAdvisor (ironic but possible), Thumbtack resource sections
- **Pitch:** "We wrote a guide on 'How to Verify Contractor Quotes'—would you link to it in your homeowner resources?"
- **Estimated success rate:** 10% (competitive but worth trying)

---

### Tier 2: Content Partnerships

#### Home Improvement Bloggers (DR 20-50)
- **Target:** DIY bloggers, renovation bloggers, home design blogs
- **Pitch:** "I noticed you wrote about [kitchen remodels]. We have detailed 2026 pricing data—would you like to reference it?"
- **Offer:** Link to their content from our blog in exchange
- **Estimated success rate:** 40%

#### Real Estate Blogs
- **Target:** First-time homebuyer guides, home seller prep blogs
- **Pitch:** "Your readers might need contractor quotes verified. Can we contribute a section or be listed as a resource?"
- **Estimated success rate:** 30%

#### Personal Finance Blogs
- **Target:** Frugal living, home budgeting, "how to save money" blogs
- **Pitch:** "We help homeowners avoid overpaying contractors—fits your audience perfectly. Mind linking?"
- **Estimated success rate:** 35%

---

### Tier 3: Guest Posts / Resource Pages

#### Target: "Best Tools for Homeowners" Lists
- **Sites:** Lifehacker, Wirecutter (NYT), Consumer Reports, Good Housekeeping
- **Pitch:** "We're a contractor quote verification tool (like Turnitin for quotes). Would you consider adding us to your 'Best Home Tools' list?"
- **Estimated success rate:** 5% (high effort, high reward)

#### Guest Post Targets
- **Sites:** The Spruce, Bob Vila, Family Handyman, This Old House
- **Pitch:** "I'd like to contribute a guide on 'How to Spot Contractor Overcharging' with data from our cost database."
- **Estimated success rate:** 10% (requires high-quality content)

## Execution Plan

### Week 1-2: Low-Hanging Fruit
- **Action:** Submit to 10-15 local business directories
- **Target:** VT, Northeast regional directories, home services directories
- **Expected backlinks:** 6-10

### Week 3-4: Blogger Outreach (Round 1)
- **Action:** Reach out to 30 home improvement bloggers
- **Target:** Bloggers who wrote about contractor costs in the last 6 months
- **Expected backlinks:** 10-12

### Week 5-6: Community Engagement
- **Action:** Engage in Reddit, Houzz, ContractorTalk
- **Target:** Provide value, mention UnGouge naturally when relevant
- **Expected backlinks:** 2-4 (from sidebar/resource sections if moderators like us)

### Week 7-8: Guest Post Pitches
- **Action:** Pitch 3 high-quality guest posts to major sites
- **Target:** The Spruce, Bob Vila, Family Handyman
- **Expected backlinks:** 0-1 (low success rate but worth trying)

**Month 1:**
- 15-20 backlinks acquired
- DR (Domain Rating) increase from 0 to 10-15

**Month 2:**
- 25-35 backlinks total
- DR 15-20
- 1-2 high-DR (50+) backlinks

**Month 3:**
- 40-50 backlinks total
- DR 20-30
- Organic traffic increase 2-3x

---

## Notes

- **Quality > quantity** - One link from NYT Wirecutter is worth 100 spammy directory links
- **Avoid black-hat tactics** - No link farms, no paid links disguised as editorial, no PBNs
- **Build relationships** - Engage genuinely with bloggers/communities before asking for links
- **Provide value first** - Answer questions, share data, be helpful—links follow naturally
