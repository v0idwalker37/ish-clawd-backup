## 5. Third-Party Service Providers

We share limited data with the following service providers, strictly as needed to operate the Service:

### 5.1 Google (Gemini API)

We use the Google Gemini API to perform AI-powered analysis of your uploaded contractor quotes. When you submit a quote:

- The content of your uploaded quote (image or PDF) and associated project details are transmitted to Google's servers for processing.
- Google processes this data pursuant to their [Google Cloud Data Processing Terms](https://cloud.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- We use the Gemini API in a configuration where Google does **not** use your data to train its general AI models.
- Data is transmitted via encrypted connections (TLS).

### 5.2 Stripe (Payment Processing)

Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe, Inc. When you make a payment:

- Your payment card details are collected and processed directly by Stripe.
- We receive only a transaction confirmation, last four digits of your card, and billing details necessary for our records.
- Stripe is PCI DSS Level 1 certified. Their privacy policy is available at [https://stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy).
- **We never see, store, or have access to your full credit card number.**

### 5.3 Other Service Providers

We may use additional service providers for:

- **Hosting and Infrastructure** (e.g., cloud hosting providers)
- **Email Delivery** (transactional emails only — not marketing)
- **Error Monitoring and Analytics** (anonymized/aggregated where possible)

All service providers are bound by contractual obligations to protect your data and use it only for the services they provide to us.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function properly, remember your preferences, and understand how you use the site.

### 8.2 Cookies We Use

### 8.3 Cookies We Do NOT Use

### 8.4 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

- **Browser Settings:** Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Consult your browser's help documentation.
- **Cookie Consent Banner:** When required by law, we display a cookie consent banner allowing you to accept or reject non-essential cookies.
- **Opt-Out Links:** Where applicable, specific opt-out mechanisms are provided.

Note: Disabling strictly necessary cookies may impair the functionality of the Service.

### 8.5 Do Not Track

We respect "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser signals. When we detect a DNT signal, we disable non-essential analytics cookies for that session.
