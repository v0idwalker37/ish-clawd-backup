# Refund Policy & Edge Cases

## Official Refund Policy (Customer-Facing)

**Simple version (for website /terms page):**

### 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If our quote analysis doesn't help you make a more informed decision, we'll refund you—no questions asked.

**To request a refund:**  
Email human@ungouge.ai within 7 days of receiving your report. Include your quote ID and reason (optional but helpful for us to improve).

**Refunds process within 2-3 business days** to your original payment method.

**What "doesn't help" means:**  
- Report was too vague or unclear  
- Analysis didn't address your specific concerns  
- You found the recommendations unhelpful  
- Technical issues prevented you from accessing the report  

**What doesn't qualify:**  
- "My contractor disagreed with your analysis" (contractors have incentive to defend their pricing)  
- "I decided not to proceed with the project" (that's a valid use of the report—it helped you decide)  
- "The report said my quote was fair but I wanted it to be lower" (we report data, not what you want to hear)

**Our goal:** Every customer should feel the $19.99 was worth it. If you don't, we want to know why—and we'll refund you.

## Internal Refund Guidelines (For Support Team)

### When to Refund Immediately (No Discussion)

1. **Report wasn't delivered** — Payment processed but report never generated → Full refund + investigate bug
2. **Technical failure** — Report is broken/unreadable/corrupted → Full refund + re-run analysis
3. **Duplicate charge** — Customer accidentally paid twice → Refund duplicate
4. **Customer explicitly unhappy** — "This didn't help me at all" → Refund, no argument, ask for feedback
5. **Within 7 days** — Any refund request within 7 days → Honor it

### When to Discuss Before Refunding

6. **"My contractor says you're wrong"** → Offer to review contractor's specific objections first, then refund if still unsatisfied
7. **"I wanted a lower quote"** → Explain we report data (not magic), but refund if they insist
8. **After 7 days** → Policy is 7 days, but be flexible for reasonable cases (email went to spam, customer was traveling, etc.)

### When to Politely Decline

9. **Report delivered, customer proceeded with project, now wants refund** (30+ days later) → "We're glad the report helped you move forward. Our policy is 7 days after delivery."
10. **Customer used report to negotiate successfully, now wants refund** → "Sounds like it saved you money! That's what we're here for. Our policy covers situations where it *didn't* help."

**Golden rule:** Err on the side of refunding. $19.99 isn't worth losing customer goodwill or getting negative reviews.

## Edge Cases & How to Handle

### Edge Case 1: Customer Got Multiple Quotes, Only One Was Analyzed

**Scenario:** Customer paid for 1 quote analysis. They have 3 quotes total. Now they want all 3 analyzed but only want to pay for 1.

**Response:**
"Each quote is $19.99. We analyze them separately because each has unique line items, pricing, and potential issues. If you want all 3 analyzed, the total is $59.97. I can offer a bulk discount: $50 for 3 quotes. Sound fair?"

**Internal note:** Offer bulk discount (3 for $50) to encourage multi-quote submissions.

---

### Edge Case 2: Contractor Changed Quote After Analysis

**Scenario:** Customer got analysis. Contractor revised quote. Customer wants new analysis without paying again.

**Response:**
"If the contractor made minor adjustments (changed a few line items), I can review it for free as a courtesy. If it's a completely different quote (new scope, different materials), that's a new analysis ($19.99). Reply with the revised quote and I'll let you know which it is."

**Internal guideline:**
- Minor changes (<20% of line items) → Free re-review
- Major changes (>20% of line items or total changed >15%) → New analysis

---

### Edge Case 3: Customer Wants Analysis Before Getting Quote

**Scenario:** "Can you tell me what a kitchen remodel should cost before I get quotes?"

**Response:**
"We can't analyze a quote that doesn't exist yet, but we have free cost guides that give you market ranges:

- [State-specific pricing](/locations/[state])
- [Kitchen remodel cost guide](/blog/kitchen-remodel-cost-2026)

These will help you spot overpricing when quotes arrive. Once you have a quote, upload it for detailed analysis."

**Don't:** Offer to "create a quote" for them. That's not our service.

---

### Edge Case 4: Customer's Contractor Wants to Debate Our Analysis

**Scenario:** "My contractor wants to talk to you directly about your analysis. Can you call him?"

**Response:**
"We don't typically engage directly with contractors (conflict of interest—we work for you, not them). But here's what I can do:

1. Ask your contractor for specific objections (which line items, which rates?)
2. Send me their response
3. I'll review and explain our reasoning or revise if they have valid points

This keeps you in control of the conversation."

**Why we don't talk to contractors directly:** They'll try to argue. We'd spend hours debating instead of helping customers.

---

### Edge Case 5: Customer Submitted Wrong Quote

**Scenario:** "I accidentally uploaded the wrong quote. Can I swap it?"

**Response:**
"No problem! If you haven't paid yet, just delete the old one and upload the new one. If you already paid, send me the correct quote and I'll analyze it instead—no extra charge."

**Internal:** Swap quotes if caught within 24 hours of payment and before analysis starts.

---

### Edge Case 6: Customer Wants Ongoing "Advisor" Service

**Scenario:** "Can I pay you a monthly fee and you review all my quotes for the next year?"

**Response:**
"We don't offer subscription plans yet, but if you're doing multiple projects, here's what I can do:

- $50 for 3 quotes (vs $60 individually)
- $150 for 10 quotes (vs $200 individually)

If you need truly ongoing advisory (weekly check-ins, strategy calls, etc.), that's outside our current scope. But I'm interested—how many quotes do you expect to get?"

**Internal note:** If 5+ customers ask for this, build a subscription tier ($50/mo for unlimited quotes + monthly advisory call).

---

### Edge Case 7: Customer Wants Us to Recommend Contractors

**Scenario:** "My quote was overpriced. Can you recommend a fair contractor in my area?"

**Response:**
"We don't refer contractors (that would make us a lead-gen site, which we're explicitly not). But here's how to find fair contractors:

1. **Ask for referrals** from neighbors/friends who recently renovated
2. **Check reviews** on Google, Yelp (look for mentions of fair pricing)
3. **Get 2-3 quotes** and upload them all—we'll tell you which is fairest

We verify pricing, not quality. You'll need to check reviews/references for quality."

**Why we don't refer:** The moment we refer contractors, we become a lead-gen platform. Our entire value prop is independence.

---

### Edge Case 8: Contractor Wants to Pay for Customer's Analysis

**Scenario:** Contractor emails: "I want to pay for my customer's quote analysis to prove my pricing is fair."

**Response:**
"Interesting offer! Here's the issue: If you pay for it, the customer might not trust the analysis (conflict of interest).

Better approach: Suggest your customer use UnGouge on their own. If our analysis says your quote is fair, that's independent validation. If it says it's high, you can adjust.

Either way, the customer trusts it more when they pay for it themselves."

**Internal:** Politely decline contractor-paid analyses. Undermines trust.

---

### Edge Case 9: Customer Wants Live Chat During Analysis

**Scenario:** "Can I talk to someone while you analyze my quote? I have questions about specific line items."

**Response:**
"Our analysis is automated and runs in the background (that's how we keep it $19.99 instead of $200+).

Once your report is ready, you can email questions and I'll clarify anything. Most reports are clear enough that customers don't need clarification, but I'm here if you do.

If you need live consultation while getting quotes, that's a different service (consider hiring a contractor consultant). We focus on data-driven analysis after quotes are in hand."

**Internal:** Don't offer live consulting. Doesn't scale. Stick to async email support.

---

### Edge Case 10: Analysis Says Fair, Customer Insists It's Not

**Scenario:** "Your report says my quote is fair, but I *know* it's overpriced. My neighbor paid way less."

**Response:**
"A few things could explain this:

1. **Different scope** — Your project might include things your neighbor's didn't (permits, higher-grade materials, more complex work)

2. **Time difference** — Material/labor costs increase 5-10% annually. A quote from 2 years ago won't match today's pricing.

3. **Different contractors** — Contractors have different overhead. A large firm with insurance, benefits, and admin costs will charge more than a solo operator.

4. **Your neighbor got a deal** — Maybe they knew the contractor, did some work themselves, or got a seasonal discount.

If you're certain our analysis is off, send me your neighbor's quote (if you can get it) and I'll compare. Or get a second quote from a different contractor and we'll analyze that too.

If you're still unsatisfied, I'm happy to refund you."

**Internal:** Refund if they push back. Don't argue. They might be right (we're 65-75% accurate, not perfect).

## Partial Refunds

**We generally don't do partial refunds.** It's either:
- Full refund (report didn't help)
- No refund (report was useful)

**Exception:** Technical glitch (report partially delivered, some sections missing) → Offer 50% refund or re-run analysis.

## Chargeback Policy

If a customer initiates a chargeback (disputes charge with their bank instead of asking us):

1. **Respond to Stripe dispute** with proof of service delivery (report PDF, email showing delivery)
2. **Email customer:** "I see you disputed the charge. If there's an issue, I'm happy to refund you directly—saves you the chargeback hassle. Was there a problem with the report?"
3. **If they don't respond:** Let Stripe handle it. We have proof we delivered.
4. **If chargeback succeeds:** Ban user from future purchases (flag account as "Chargeback - Do Not Serve")

**Why:** Chargebacks cost us $15+ in fees. If they wanted a refund, they should've asked.

## FAQ: Refund Policy

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**  
A: Yes. 7-day money-back guarantee if the report doesn't help you.

**Q: What if I just changed my mind?**  
A: If it's within 7 days and you haven't used the report, we'll refund you.

**Q: What if my contractor says your analysis is wrong?**  
A: We'll review their specific objections. If we agree, we'll revise the report. If we disagree but you're unsatisfied, we'll refund you.

**Q: What if the report was delivered late?**  
A: We'll refund you if it was our fault (system issue, AI failure, etc.). If it was a payment delay or upload issue on your end, we won't refund but we'll still deliver the report.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I've already used the report to negotiate?**  
A: If it helped you negotiate, that's a successful outcome! Our refund policy is for cases where the report *didn't* help. But if you're truly unsatisfied, reach out—we'll discuss.

## Summary

**Our refund philosophy:** Be generous. $19.99 isn't worth a bad review or unhappy customer. Refund when in doubt.

**Exceptions:** Obvious abuse (customer used report successfully, then asks for refund 30 days later) or fraud (chargeback instead of asking for refund).

**Track refunds closely.** If >5% of customers refund, we're not delivering value. Fix the product, not the policy.
