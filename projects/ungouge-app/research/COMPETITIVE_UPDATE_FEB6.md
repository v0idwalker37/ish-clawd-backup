## BidCompareAI (GreatBuildz) — Main Competitor

### Recent Press Coverage (Aug-Sep 2025)
- CCR-Mag: "AI Tool Helps Homeowners Cut Renovation Costs"
- Resident.com: "Thwarting Home Renovation Regret"
- WTOP News: "Let AI take a look" 
- UExpress Housing Scene

### How It Works
- Upload **multiple** contractor bids
- AI compares them **against each other**
- Generates report: scope differences, pricing gaps, red flags, inconsistencies
- **FREE** — no sign-up, no payment required

### Their Business Model
BidCompareAI is a **lead funnel** for GreatBuildz:
- GreatBuildz is a contractor referral service
- Free tool → capture user contact info → connect with contractors
- Classic lead-gen play disguised as a free tool

### Our Differentiation

| Feature | BidCompareAI | UnGouge |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| Price | Free | $19.99/report |
| What it compares | Multiple bids vs each other | Single quote vs market data |
| Data source | User's own bids only | Independent market research |
| Business model | Lead generation | Direct payment |
| User data | Sold to contractors | Never sold |
| Value prop | "Which bid is better?" | "Is this quote fair?" |

### Key Messaging Opportunity

**BidCompareAI's weakness:** Only as good as the bids you have. If all three contractors are overcharging, BidCompareAI won't catch it — it'll just tell you which overpriced bid is cheapest.

**UnGouge's strength:** We compare against **independent market data**, not just your other bids. Even with one quote, we can tell you if you're being gouged.

**Tagline angle:** "What if all your bids are too high?"
