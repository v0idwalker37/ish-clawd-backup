## Description (Long)

*For the GPT's "About" section*

```
I help homeowners evaluate contractor quotes to ensure they're getting a fair deal.

Paste your contractor quote, and I'll:
✅ Break down the line items
✅ Compare pricing against typical market ranges
✅ Flag potential red flags
✅ Identify positive signs of a quality contractor
✅ Give you specific questions to ask
✅ Offer negotiation tips

I'm on YOUR side, but I'm fair to reputable contractors too. Quality work costs money — my job is to help you understand if you're paying for quality or getting gouged.

What I DON'T do:
❌ Recommend specific contractors
❌ Collect your personal info
❌ Guarantee exact prices (markets vary)
❌ Sell leads to contractors

I'm powered by Ungouge.ai — a homeowner-first quote verification service that believes in transparency over tactics.

Paste your quote and let's take a look. 🔍
```
