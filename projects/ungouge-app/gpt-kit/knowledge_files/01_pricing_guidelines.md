## How Contractor Pricing Works

### The Standard Markup Structure
Most contractors price jobs using this formula:

```
Total Price = (Materials × Markup) + (Labor Hours × Hourly Rate) + Overhead + Profit
```

Typical breakdown for a $10,000 job:
- Materials: 25-35% ($2,500-3,500)
- Labor: 30-40% ($3,000-4,000)
- Overhead: 10-15% ($1,000-1,500)
- Profit: 15-25% ($1,500-2,500)

### Regional Price Variations
Prices vary significantly by location:

| Region | Cost Multiplier |
|--------|----------------|
| Northeast (NYC, Boston) | 1.3-1.5x |
| West Coast (SF, LA, Seattle) | 1.3-1.6x |
| Midwest (Chicago, Detroit) | 0.9-1.1x |
| South (Atlanta, Dallas, Miami) | 0.9-1.2x |
| Rural areas | 0.7-0.9x |

**Note:** Always ask for the user's general location to calibrate expectations.

### Seasonal Variations
- Spring/Summer: Peak season, prices 10-15% higher, longer wait times
- Fall: Sweet spot — good weather, lower demand
- Winter: Cheapest for indoor work, avoid outdoor projects

## Project-Specific Pricing Ranges

### ROOFING

**Full Replacement (Asphalt Shingles)**
| Component | Low | Average | High |
|-----------|-----|---------|------|
| Per square (100 sq ft) | $350 | $500 | $800 |
| Average home (20-25 squares) | $7,000 | $12,500 | $20,000 |
| Tear-off & disposal | $1,000 | $2,000 | $4,000 |
| Underlayment upgrade | $500 | $1,200 | $2,500 |

**Material Tiers:**
- 3-tab shingles: Cheapest, 15-20 year warranty
- Architectural/dimensional: Mid-range, 25-30 year warranty
- Premium (designer, impact-resistant): Highest, 40-50 year warranty

**Red flags in roofing quotes:**
- No mention of ice & water shield in cold climates
- "Per square" math doesn't add up to total
- Reusing old flashing
- No permit mention for full replacement

### HVAC

**Central AC Replacement**
| Component | Low | Average | High |
|-----------|-----|---------|------|
| 2.5-3 ton unit installed | $4,000 | $6,500 | $10,000 |
| Furnace replacement | $3,000 | $5,000 | $8,000 |
| Full system (AC + Furnace) | $7,000 | $12,000 | $18,000 |
| Ductwork modification | $500 | $2,000 | $5,000 |

**SEER Ratings (Efficiency):**
- 14-15 SEER: Entry level, meets minimum code
- 16-18 SEER: Mid-efficiency, good balance
- 19+ SEER: High-efficiency, higher upfront, lower bills

**Red flags in HVAC quotes:**
- Not performing Manual J load calculation
- Oversizing or undersizing without explanation
- No mention of refrigerant line replacement when needed
- Extremely low price (may be unlicensed)

## When Prices Are Justified to Be Higher

- Difficult access (tight spaces, upper floors, steep roofs)
- Older homes with unforeseen issues
- Premium materials with longer warranties
- Licensed, bonded, insured with good reviews
- Peak season / high demand
- Small jobs (minimum charges apply)
- Code upgrades required to bring up to current standards

## When Prices Should Be Lower

- Large volume / whole-house projects
- Off-season scheduling
- Flexible timeline
- Simple, straightforward access
- Builder-grade materials
- Repeat customer
