## What This GPT Does

**For homeowners:**
- Analyzes pasted contractor quotes
- Flags red flags (pressure tactics, missing details, etc.)
- Compares pricing to typical market ranges
- Provides negotiation scripts
- Recommends questions to ask

**For Ungouge:**
- Top-of-funnel lead magnet
- Builds brand awareness
- Soft CTA to ungouge.ai for full reports
- Positions us as the expert

---

## What This GPT Does NOT Do

- ❌ Recommend specific contractors (no lead gen)
- ❌ Collect user personal data
- ❌ Guarantee exact prices
- ❌ Replace professional estimates
