## Thread 2: How Contractors Price Jobs

**Tweet 1:**
Ever wonder how contractors actually price jobs?

Here's the formula most use — and how to spot when it's way off.

🧵

**Tweet 2:**
The basic formula:

Materials × 1.15-1.3 (markup)
+ Labor hours × loaded rate
+ Overhead (trucks, insurance, office)
+ Profit margin (10-20%)

Legitimate contractors run 40-60% gross margins. More than that? Ask questions.

**Tweet 3:**
"Loaded rate" = what labor actually costs the company.

A worker paid $30/hr costs ~$50/hr with:
- Workers comp
- Payroll taxes
- Insurance
- Benefits

So a 3-person crew for 8 hours ≈ $1,200 in labor, not $720.

**Tweet 4:**
Red flags in quotes:

❌ Round numbers ($15,000 exactly)
❌ No line-item breakdown
❌ Verbal quote only
❌ "This price is only good today"
❌ Asking for large deposits upfront

**Tweet 5:**
Green flags:

✅ Itemized materials with quantities
✅ Labor broken out by task
✅ Clear payment schedule
✅ Written contract
✅ Willing to explain line items
