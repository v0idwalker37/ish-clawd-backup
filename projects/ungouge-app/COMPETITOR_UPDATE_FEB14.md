# Competitor Landscape Update — Feb 14, 2026

## BidCompare AI (GreatBuildz)
- **URL:** greatbuildz.com/bidcompare-ai/
- **Status:** Live, getting press coverage (KTVO news, Jan 2026)
- **Model:** FREE tool — upload bids, get analysis via email
- **What they offer:** Bid comparison spreadsheet, analysis report, "expert analysis"
- **Revenue model:** LEAD GENERATION — GreatBuildz is a contractor matching service
  - They help homeowners "find trusted contractors" → they sell leads to contractors
  - BidCompare AI is a funnel to capture homeowner data + project details
  - "Free service" because YOU are the product
- **Their CTA:** "Schedule a call" and "Call Now – 818.317.3567" (contractor matching sales)
- **Press:** Co-founder Jon Grishpul getting TV coverage on local news stations
- **Weakness:** It's a marketing tool disguised as a helpful tool. Analysis quality unknown.

## Key Takeaways
1. **BidCompare AI is our only real competitor** and they're getting press
2. Their free model is funded by lead gen — our $19.99 model is the honest alternative
3. They need multiple bids (comparison tool); we work with a SINGLE quote (verification tool)
4. Different value props: they help you CHOOSE between bids; we tell you if a bid is FAIR
5. Their press coverage validates the market — homeowners WANT this tool
6. Our positioning is stronger: "We make $19.99 when you pay us. That's it."
7. We should reference them (without naming) in marketing: "Other tools are free because they sell your data to contractors"
