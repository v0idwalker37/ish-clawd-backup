# AI/Human Task Allocation Matrix - UnGouge Partnership Model

**Philosophy:** AI handles execution and scale. Jason handles strategy, personal brand, and final judgment.  
**Goal:** Build a $350K-500K/year business in 16 hours/week (Jason's time)  
**Model:** AI is the operator, Jason is the CEO

---

## High-Level Division

| Responsibility | AI % | Jason % | Rationale |
|----------------|------|---------|-----------|
| **Content Creation** | 80% | 20% | AI drafts, Jason edits/approves for voice + expertise |
| **Social Media** | 70% | 30% | AI creates content, Jason adds personal engagement |
| **Marketing Strategy** | 40% | 60% | AI proposes, Jason decides |
| **Product Development** | 50% | 50% | AI implements, Jason directs features |
| **Customer Support** | 60% | 40% | AI handles routine, Jason handles complex/relationship |
| **Analytics** | 90% | 10% | AI pulls/analyzes data, Jason interprets and acts |
| **Business Operations** | 30% | 70% | Jason owns financial, legal, strategic decisions |

**Overall Split:** AI handles ~65% of total work, Jason handles ~35% (but 35% = higher leverage)

## Scaling Strategy (As Revenue Grows)

### At $10K MRR (~$120K/year):
- **Hire:** Part-time VA for Tier 1 support (5-10 hrs/week)
- **Jason time saved:** 1-2 hrs/week
- **Cost:** $500-800/month
- **ROI:** Frees Jason for higher-leverage work

### At $25K MRR (~$300K/year):
- **Hire:** Full-time content manager/community manager
- **Jason time saved:** 5-8 hrs/week (social media delegation)
- **Cost:** $3K-5K/month
- **ROI:** Jason focuses on product, partnerships, strategy

### At $40K MRR (~$480K/year):
- **Hire:** Product manager or developer
- **Jason time saved:** 2-3 hrs/week (technical delegation)
- **Cost:** $5K-8K/month
- **ROI:** Faster product iteration

**Philosophy:** Only hire when Jason's time becomes the bottleneck, not before.

## Decision Framework: "Should AI or Jason Handle This?"

### AI Should Handle If:
- ✅ It's repeatable and scalable
- ✅ It's based on data/research
- ✅ It can be templated
- ✅ Quality can be reviewed/approved later
- ✅ It saves Jason 30+ minutes

### Jason Should Handle If:
- ✅ It requires personal judgment or experience
- ✅ It's relationship-building (customers, partners)
- ✅ It's strategic (roadmap, pricing, vision)
- ✅ It involves the brand voice or reputation
- ✅ It's legally/financially sensitive

**When in doubt:** AI drafts, Jason reviews. Fastest path to quality at scale.
