# SEO Keyword Research - UnGouge

**Last Updated:** 2026-02-03  
**Strategy:** Target homeowners actively seeking contractor pricing information and validation

## Keyword Strategy Overview

### Tiered Approach:

**Tier 1 (High Competition, High Volume):**
- Focus: Broad awareness, competitive differentiation
- Content: Comparison posts, educational guides
- Timeline: Months 4-12 (build authority first)

**Tier 2 (Medium Competition, Qualified Traffic):**
- Focus: Problem-aware homeowners researching quotes
- Content: Detailed how-to guides, cost breakdowns
- Timeline: Months 1-6 (priority focus)

**Tier 3 (Low Competition, High Intent):**
- Focus: Ready-to-analyze quotes, immediate need
- Content: Tool-focused landing pages, specific scenarios
- Timeline: Month 1 (quick wins)
