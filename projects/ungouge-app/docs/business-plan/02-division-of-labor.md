## Overview

Jason has 15-20 hours/week available. This document maps every task to one of four categories:

- 🤖 **AI/Sub-Agent Fully Owns** — Jason reviews output, gives thumbs up/down
- 🤝 **Collaborative** — AI drafts, Jason provides input/decisions, AI finalizes
- 👤 **Jason Executes** — Only Jason can do this (accounts, legal, human judgment)
- ⏰ **Estimated Hours** — Jason's time investment per phase
