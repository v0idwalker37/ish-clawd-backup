# ungouge.ai - Business Brief

**One-page overview for stakeholders, partners, and potential customers.**

---

## 🎯 The Problem

**Businesses lose thousands annually to overpriced vendor quotes.**

- Small businesses lack procurement expertise to spot inflated pricing
- Comparing quotes manually is time-consuming and error-prone
- Market research for every line item is impractical
- Vendors know most customers won't negotiate aggressively

**Result:** Companies routinely overpay 20-50% on IT, equipment, and services.

---

## 💡 The Solution

**ungouge.ai: AI-powered quote analysis in 60 seconds.**

1. **Upload** any vendor quote (PDF, image, or text)
2. **AI extracts** line items and pricing automatically
3. **Benchmark** against real market data
4. **Get** a gouging score + specific negotiation tactics
5. **Save** thousands per purchase

---

## 🎬 How It Works

```
User uploads quote → AI parses document → Match items to market prices → 
Calculate markup → Generate report with savings opportunities → 
User negotiates with confidence
```

**Example Output:**
- **Gouging Score:** 68/100 (High Risk)
- **Potential Savings:** $12,400 (28% of quote)
- **Top Recommendation:** "Dell PowerEdge R740 is marked up 45% above CDW pricing. Ask vendor to match $8,200 or source elsewhere."

---

## 🎯 Target Customer

**Primary:** Small-to-medium businesses (10-500 employees)

- Office managers handling equipment purchases

**Secondary:** Consultants and advisors
- IT consultants reviewing client quotes

**Pain Points:**

## 🛠️ Tech Overview

**Stack:** Next.js + PostgreSQL + OpenAI + Anthropic + Stripe  
**Hosting:** Vercel + Supabase (serverless, scales automatically)  
**AI Models:**
- GPT-4o Vision: Quote parsing (OCR + extraction)
- Claude 3.5 Sonnet: Analysis + recommendations

**Build Time:** 8-12 weeks to MVP  
**Team:** 1 full-stack dev + 1 AI/data specialist (or solo founder)
