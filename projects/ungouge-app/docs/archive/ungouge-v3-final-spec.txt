UNGOUGE.AI PROJECT SUMMARY — Full Context for Continuation
What We're Building
Ungouge.ai is a paid consumer utility ($49/report) that audits contractor quotes for homeowners. Users upload a quote (PDF/image), AI extracts line items via Gemini Vision, compares them against fair-market benchmarks adjusted for their location, and generates a report flagging overcharges.
Key differentiator: Anti-lead-gen positioning. We never sell user data. No contractor referrals. Pure consumer utility. "We make $49 when you pay us. That's it."

Hybrid Pricing Strategy (Key Innovation)
Instead of requiring $4k/year Craftsman license at launch:

Extract Top 50 most common items (roofing, HVAC, painting, etc.) from estimator book — national averages
Apply regional multipliers by zip code (NYC = 1.35x, Rural South = 0.80x, etc.) using free BLS data
Items not in Top 50 get flagged as "unverified" — honest about limitations
Upgrade to Craftsman when revenue supports it (~50+ reports/month)

Formula: Adjusted Fair Price = National Benchmark × Regional Multiplier
