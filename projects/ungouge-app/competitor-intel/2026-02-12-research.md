- Baltimore Sun article (Feb 3, 2026): "AI chatbot cannot generate accurate contractor quote"
- His stance: AI can't replace experienced contractor insight
- His business model: Phone coaching ($100+ per call)
- **IMPORTANT:** His example proves our use case — homeowner got a $4,300 quote for 30 sqft wood rot repair + tile replacement, went to ChatGPT, ChatGPT said "too high" but couldn't explain why
- Tim's value: Detailed line-item breakdown showing the quote was actually reasonable
- **Our advantage:** We do EXACTLY what Tim does — detailed cost breakdown with transparency — for $19.99 vs $100+ phone call

- Quote: "No one aspect of the job is a big number. The trouble is, all the small numbers add up to a big one."
