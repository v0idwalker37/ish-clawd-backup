# UnGouge Digest - Content Calendar (Q1 2024)

## Content Strategy Framework

### Content Pillars
1. **Price Breakdowns** - "Real Cost" series for major projects
2. **Quote Analysis** - Tear apart actual homeowner quotes (anonymized)
3. **Red Flags** - Warning signs of gouging/scams
4. **DIY vs Hire** - Decision frameworks for different projects
5. **Regional Pricing** - Geographic cost variations
6. **Industry Secrets** - Contractor interviews, insider knowledge
7. **Myth Busting** - Common homeowner misconceptions

### Content Mix Target
- **40%** Evergreen (always relevant, searchable)
- **30%** Timely (seasonal, trending topics)
- **20%** Investigative (deeper dives, original research)
- **10%** Community (Q&A, viewer quote analysis)
