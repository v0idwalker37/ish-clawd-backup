# UnGouge Digest - Production Workflow

## Philosophy

**Goal:** Produce high-quality, data-driven videos efficiently by leveraging AI (Ish) for research, scripting, and editing prep while Jason focuses on presentation, filming, and final creative decisions.

**Target:** 2 videos per week, 10-15 minutes each, with minimal burnout.

---

## Team Roles

### Ish (AI Assistant) Responsibilities
- Research & data collection
- Script drafting and fact-checking
- Source citation compilation
- Thumbnail concept design
- SEO optimization (titles, descriptions, tags)
- Video outline structuring
- B-roll shot list creation
- Editing notes and chapter markers

### Jason (Host/Producer) Responsibilities
- Topic selection and prioritization
- On-camera presentation
- Filming (A-roll and B-roll)
- Final script review and personalization
- Video editing (or delegation to editor)
- Thumbnail creation (using Ish's templates)
- Publishing and community management
- Strategic decisions and brand voice

---

## Step-by-Step Workflow

### Phase 1: Topic Selection & Research
**Timeline:** 3-5 days before filming

#### Step 1.1: Jason Selects Topic
**Time:** 15 minutes  
**Process:**
- Review content calendar (`content-calendar.md`)
- Select next episode based on:
  - Seasonal relevance
  - Viewer requests/comments
  - Trending search terms
  - Personal expertise/interest
- Send topic to Ish via message or task file

**Output:** Topic brief (e.g., "HVAC Replacement Real Costs - focus on 2024 pricing, compare big brands vs. local contractors")

---

#### Step 1.2: Ish Conducts Deep Research
**Time:** 2-4 hours  
**Process:**
1. **Price Data Collection:**
   - Wholesale equipment/material costs (manufacturer sites, supply houses)
   - Retail pricing (Home Depot, Lowe's, specialty retailers)
   - Labor rates by region (HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, contractor surveys)
   - Permit costs (local jurisdiction websites)

2. **Source Verification:**
   - Cross-reference 3+ sources for each data point
   - Note date/currency of data (prices change!)
   - Save source URLs and access dates

3. **Trend Analysis:**
   - Historical pricing (if available)
   - Regional variations (Northeast vs. Southeast, urban vs. rural)
   - Seasonal fluctuations

4. **Case Study Collection:**
   - Real homeowner quotes (from viewer submissions or online forums)
   - Contractor interviews (if available)
   - BBB complaints or success stories

**Output:** Research document with:
- Itemized cost breakdowns (materials, labor, overhead, profit)
- Source citations (MLA or APA format)
- Regional comparison tables
- Key statistics and quotes
- Red flags or common gouging patterns

**Tools:**
- Web search and fetch
- Spreadsheet software (for cost calculations)
- Note-taking app with source links

**Quality Check:**
- [ ] All prices verified from 2+ sources?
- [ ] Sources dated within last 6 months?
- [ ] Regional data includes viewer's likely locations?
- [ ] Clear differentiation between fair pricing and gouge?

---

#### Step 1.3: Jason Reviews Research
**Time:** 30 minutes  
**Process:**
- Read through Ish's research doc
- Flag any questionable data or missing context
- Add personal anecdotes or local knowledge
- Confirm angle/narrative for video

**Output:** Approved research with notes for script direction

---

### Phase 2: Scripting & Pre-Production
**Timeline:** 2-3 days before filming

#### Step 2.1: Ish Drafts Script
**Time:** 2-3 hours  
**Process:**
1. **Structure:**
   - Hook (0:00-0:15): Attention-grabbing stat or question
   - Context (0:15-1:00): Why this matters, who it affects
   - Main Content (1:00-9:00): Price breakdowns, comparisons, analysis
   - Call to Action (9:00-10:00): Recap, next steps, subscribe

2. **Writing Style:**
   - Conversational tone (write how Jason speaks)
   - Short sentences and paragraphs (easier to read on camera)
   - Include pauses and emphasis cues: **(pause)**, **[emphasis]**
   - Avoid jargon without explanation

3. **Data Integration:**
   - Present numbers in visual-friendly formats (round to nearest $100)
   - Use comparisons ("That's like paying for a new car every 5 years")
   - Cite sources naturally ("According to HomeAdvisor's 2024 survey...")

4. **Visual Cues:**
   - Mark where graphics/B-roll should appear: **[GRAPHIC: Price comparison table]**
   - Suggest on-screen text: **[ON SCREEN: $18,500 → $9,200]**

**Output:** Full script draft (Google Doc or markdown file) with:
- Timestamp estimates
- Visual cue notes
- Source citations as footnotes
- Alternative phrasing options for key points

**Tools:**
- LLM for drafting
- Grammar check (Grammarly or similar)
- Readability analysis (aim for 8th-grade reading level for spoken clarity)

**Quality Check:**
- [ ] Hook grabs attention in first 15 seconds?
- [ ] Main thesis clear and repeated?
- [ ] Data presented visually (not just spoken)?
- [ ] Conversational, not academic?
- [ ] Call to action specific and compelling?

---

#### Step 2.2: Jason Reviews & Personalizes Script
**Time:** 1-2 hours  
**Process:**
- Read script aloud (sounds different than reading silently)
- Rewrite stiff or unnatural phrasing
- Add personal stories or humor
- Trim fat (aim for <15 min final video)
- Mark sections to ad-lib vs. stick to script

**Output:** Final shooting script with Jason's voice

---

#### Step 2.3: Ish Creates B-Roll Shot List
**Time:** 30 minutes  
**Process:**
Based on script, list every B-roll shot needed:
- Product/material close-ups
- Website screenshots (price comparisons)
- Document shots (sample quotes, invoices)
- Tool or workspace shots
- Screen recordings (calculator, spreadsheet)

**Output:** B-roll checklist organized by location (desk, workshop, computer screen)

---

#### Step 2.4: Ish Designs Thumbnail Concept
**Time:** 45 minutes  
**Process:**
- Select template from `thumbnail-templates.md`
- Draft text overlay (headline + supporting text)
- Specify background image or color
- Note any face/reaction shot needed from Jason

**Output:** Thumbnail brief (text description or rough mockup in Canva)

**Example:**
```
Template: Price Shock Split
Left side (red): "CONTRACTOR QUOTE: $18,500"
Right side (green): "REAL COST: $9,200"
Bottom: "HVAC REPLACEMENT"
Background: Photo of HVAC unit or Jason shocked face (left 40%)
```

---

### Phase 3: Filming
**Timeline:** Filming day

#### Step 3.1: Jason Films A-Roll (Main Footage)
**Time:** 1-2 hours  
**Process:**
1. **Setup:**
   - Camera: Tripod-mounted, eye level, rule of thirds framing
   - Lighting: 3-point lighting (key, fill, back)
   - Audio: Lavalier mic or shotgun mic, monitor levels
   - Background: Clean, branded, consistent

2. **Filming:**
   - Record entire script in 2-3 takes (full run-throughs)
   - Film additional "pickup" shots for key stats or emphasis
   - Record multiple thumbnail reaction shots (10-15 variations)

3. **Review:**
   - Watch playback to catch any major mistakes
   - Re-film sections if needed

**Tools:**
- Camera (DSLR, mirrorless, or high-quality smartphone)
- Tripod
- Lighting kit
- Microphone
- Teleprompter (optional, but helps with pacing)

**Quality Check:**
- [ ] Audio clean (no background noise)?
- [ ] Focus sharp, exposure correct?
- [ ] Framing consistent across takes?
- [ ] Thumbnail shots captured?

---

#### Step 3.2: Jason Films B-Roll
**Time:** 30-60 minutes  
**Process:**
- Follow Ish's B-roll shot list
- Capture:
  - Close-ups of materials, tools, documents
  - Screen recordings (price comparisons, websites)
  - "Filler" footage (hands typing, writing, pointing at screen)
  - Establishing shots (if on location)

**Tips:**
- Film 10-15 seconds per shot (gives editing flexibility)
- Vary angles (overhead, side, close-up)
- Keep camera stable (tripod or gimbal)

---

### Phase 4: Editing & Post-Production
**Timeline:** 1-3 days after filming

#### Step 4.1: Jason Edits Video (or Delegates)
**Time:** 3-5 hours (first few videos); 2-3 hours (once templated)  
**Process:**
1. **Rough Cut:**
   - Import footage into editing software
   - Lay down best A-roll take
   - Cut out mistakes, long pauses, "ums"
   - Trim to target length (10-15 min)

2. **B-Roll Integration:**
   - Add B-roll over sections where Jason discusses specific items
   - Ensure B-roll covers all jump cuts (for smooth flow)

3. **Graphics & Text:**
   - Add on-screen stats, price comparisons (as noted in script)
   - Animated lower-thirds for citations: "Source: HomeAdvisor 2024"
   - Chapter markers (every 2-3 min for YouTube chapters)

4. **Audio:**
   - Balance levels (voice clear, no distortion)
   - Add subtle background music (non-intrusive, royalty-free)
   - Sound effects (optional: "cha-ching" for savings, etc.)

5. **Color Grading:**
   - Basic correction for consistent look
   - Slight saturation boost for visual appeal

**Tools:**
- DaVinci Resolve (free, professional)
- Adobe Premiere Pro (subscription, industry standard)
- Final Cut Pro (Mac, one-time purchase)
- CapCut (free, beginner-friendly)

**Quality Check:**
- [ ] Video flows smoothly, no jarring cuts?
- [ ] All key stats have on-screen graphics?
- [ ] Audio levels consistent throughout?
- [ ] Chapter markers added (for YouTube)?
- [ ] Intro/outro consistent with brand?

---

#### Step 4.2: Ish Reviews Edit (Optional)
**Time:** 30 minutes  
**Process:**
- Jason shares rough cut (unlisted YouTube link or video file)
- Ish watches and notes:
  - Factual errors or misstatements
  - Missing citations
  - Sections that drag or confuse
  - SEO opportunities (verbal CTAs, on-screen text)

**Output:** Timestamped feedback notes

---

#### Step 4.3: Jason Finalizes Edit
**Time:** 30-60 minutes  
**Process:**
- Implement Ish's feedback (if applicable)
- Final polish (color, audio, pacing)
- Export video:
  - Resolution: 1080p minimum (4K if possible)
  - Format: MP4 (H.264 codec)
  - Frame rate: 24fps or 30fps (consistent across all videos)

---

### Phase 5: Optimization & Publishing
**Timeline:** Publishing day

#### Step 5.1: Ish Prepares SEO Assets
**Time:** 1 hour  
**Process:**
1. **Video Title:**
   - Include target keyword (from content calendar)
   - Front-load key terms ("HVAC Replacement Cost 2024")
   - Add curiosity hook ("Real Price vs. What They Charge")
   - Keep under 60 characters for mobile display

2. **Description (5000 char max):**
   - First 150 chars: Hook + keyword (this shows in search previews)
   - Timestamps for chapters
   - Source links (all citations from research)
   - Relevant affiliate disclaimers (if any)
   - Social media links
   - Call to action (subscribe, comment)

3. **Tags:**
   - Use tags from `channel-branding.md`
   - Add episode-specific long-tail keywords
   - Include 15-20 tags total

4. **Thumbnail Text:**
   - Finalize exact wording for thumbnail
   - Ensure mobile readability (test at 320px wide)

**Output:** Publishing checklist with all text assets ready to copy-paste

**Example Title:**
```
HVAC Replacement Cost 2024: $9K vs. $18K Quote Breakdown
```

**Example Description (first 150 chars):**
```
HVAC companies charge $18K for systems that cost $9K. Here's the real breakdown of materials, labor, and fair profit—so you don't get gouged.

🕒 CHAPTERS
0:00 - Intro
0:35 - What HVAC Companies Actually Pay
2:15 - Fair Labor Costs
4:30 - Regional Price Differences
...
```

---

#### Step 5.2: Jason Creates Final Thumbnail
**Time:** 30-45 minutes  
**Process:**
- Open thumbnail template (Canva or Photoshop)
- Insert reaction shot (if using)
- Add text per Ish's spec
- Export as JPG, <2MB
- Preview at mobile size (squint test!)

**Quality Check:**
- [ ] Text readable at thumbnail size?
- [ ] Colors on-brand?
- [ ] Face (if included) making clear emotional expression?
- [ ] Saved at 1280x720px?

---

#### Step 5.3: Jason Uploads & Publishes
**Time:** 20 minutes  
**Process:**
1. **Upload to YouTube:**
   - Drag video file into YouTube Studio
   - While uploading, paste title, description, tags (from Ish's doc)
   - Upload thumbnail
   - Set visibility: Public (or Scheduled for consistent publish time)

2. **Advanced Settings:**
   - Category: Howto & Style (or Education)
   - Enable "Not made for kids"
   - Enable comments (moderate if needed)
   - Add to "UnGouge Digest" playlist
   - Enable auto-chapters (or manually add from description)

3. **End Screens & Cards:**
   - Add subscribe button (right side, last 20 seconds)
   - Link to related video (left side, last 20 seconds)
   - Add mid-roll card at key CTA points

4. **Publish:**
   - Double-check everything (title, thumbnail, description)
   - Click "Publish"

**Post-Publishing:**
- Share on social media (Twitter, Facebook, Reddit r/homeimprovement)
- Post in UnGouge Digest community tab
- Pin comment asking for viewer quotes/experiences

---

### Phase 6: Analytics & Iteration
**Timeline:** 48 hours after publishing, then weekly

#### Step 6.1: Ish Monitors Performance
**Time:** 30 minutes (48hrs after publish), then 15 min weekly  
**Process:**
- Track YouTube Studio analytics:
  - Views, CTR (click-through rate), watch time
  - Audience retention graph (where people drop off)
  - Traffic sources (search, suggested, external)
  - Top search terms that led to video

**Output:** Performance report with:
- What worked (high CTR thumbnail, strong retention at X timestamp)
- What didn't (drop-off at Y point, low search traffic)
- Recommendations (adjust future scripts/thumbnails based on data)

---

#### Step 6.2: Jason Reviews Analytics & Adjusts
**Time:** 30 minutes weekly  
**Process:**
- Read Ish's performance report
- Compare to previous videos
- Identify patterns:
  - Do price comparison videos perform better than deep dives?
  - Which thumbnails get higher CTR?
  - Where do viewers drop off? (Too long intros? Boring middle sections?)

**Output:** Notes for next video improvements

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Problem: Research takes too long
**Solution:**
- Create reusable pricing databases (e.g., "HVAC brands & wholesale costs")
- Interview contractors upfront for baseline labor rates
- Build template research docs for common project types

### Problem: Jason sounds scripted on camera
**Solution:**
- Use bullet-point teleprompter instead of full script
- Film "off-the-cuff" takes, then tighten in editing
- Practice script multiple times before filming

### Problem: Editing takes forever
**Solution:**
- Create editing templates (intro/outro pre-built)
- Use keyboard shortcuts religiously
- Batch similar tasks (color grade all clips at once)
- Accept "good enough" over "perfect"

### Problem: Low views despite good content
**Solution:**
- Ish runs deeper SEO analysis (competitor keywords, trending searches)
- A/B test thumbnails (YouTube allows thumbnail changes)
- Promote in niche communities (Reddit, Facebook groups)
- Collaborate with other homeowner YouTubers
