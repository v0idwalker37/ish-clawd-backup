# UnGouge Digest - Channel Branding

## Channel Name Options

### 1. **UnGouge Digest** (Working Title)
**Pros:**
- Clear advocacy positioning - immediately signals homeowner protection
- "Digest" implies curated, essential information
- Memorable and provocative
- Good SEO for "gouge" + home improvement searches
- Unique in the space

**Cons:**
- Negative framing might limit appeal to casual DIYers
- Could sound confrontational to some viewers
- "Gouge" is somewhat informal

**Verdict:** Strong contender. Direct, mission-driven, stands out.

---

### 2. **Fair Price Home**
**Pros:**
- Positive framing (what you get, not what you avoid)
- Broader appeal to general homeowners
- Clear value proposition
- Easy to remember and spell
- Domain likely available

**Cons:**
- Less distinctive in crowded home improvement space
- Could be mistaken for a real estate channel
- Less emotional punch than UnGouge
- Doesn't convey advocacy edge

**Verdict:** Safe, accessible, but less memorable.

---

### 3. **The Homeowner Advocate**
**Pros:**
- Professional, authoritative tone
- Clear positioning as consumer protection
- Broad enough to cover all topics
- Appeals to homeowners who want expert guidance
- Strong for building trust

**Cons:**
- Generic sounding
- Less catchy/viral potential
- Longer name (less punchy for branding)
- Could sound dry or boring

**Verdict:** Credible but lacks personality.

---

### 4. **Real Cost: Home Edition**
**Pros:**
- Implies transparency and truth-telling
- "Real" signals authenticity (YouTube-friendly)
- Specific niche focus
- Good hook for price comparison content
- Scalable format ("Real Cost: X Edition")

**Cons:**
- Format feels derivative (many "Real Cost" channels)
- Less emotional connection
- Doesn't emphasize advocacy
- Could limit content variety

**Verdict:** Solid concept but not distinctive enough.

---

### 5. **Honest Homeowner**
**Pros:**
- Alliterative (memorable)
- Positions you as the honest voice vs. dishonest contractors
- Friendly, approachable tone
- Works for both advocacy and DIY content
- Great for building personal brand

**Cons:**
- Very common words (harder SEO)
- Could sound preachy
- Less clear about specific mission
- Might attract conspiracy theorists

**Verdict:** Likeable but unfocused.

---

## **Recommendation:** UnGouge Digest
The working title is the strongest. It's distinctive, mission-driven, and immediately communicates value to the target audience. The slight edge makes it memorable in a crowded space.

## Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines

**Voice Attributes:**
- **Advocate, not agitator:** Firm and data-driven, but not angry or conspiratorial
- **Empowering:** "Here's what you need to know" not "You're screwed"
- **Accessible:** Explain jargon, assume viewers are smart but not experts
- **Trustworthy:** Always cite sources, show your work, admit uncertainties

**What to Avoid:**
- Contractor-bashing without evidence
- Fear-mongering or clickbait rage
- Oversimplifying complex pricing factors
- Pretending all contractors are crooked

**Tone Examples:**

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use |
|---------|--------|
| "Contractors are SCAMMING you!" | "Here's how to spot inflated pricing." |
| "You're an idiot if you pay this." | "Most homeowners don't know to ask these questions." |
| "This is a TOTAL ripoff!!!" | "This quote has $8K in unnecessary markup. Let me show you." |
