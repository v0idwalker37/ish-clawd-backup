## Recording Requirements for Professional Voice Clone

### Audio Length
- **Bare minimum:** 30 minutes of clear audio
- **Recommended:** 2-3 hours of audio
- **Ideal approach:** Don't record 2 hours straight - record in 10-30 minute sessions, then combine

### Audio Quality Standards
The AI clones EVERYTHING it hears, so quality is critical:

**Required:**
- No background noise (AC, traffic, other people, music)
- No room echo/reverb
- Single speaker only (just you)
- Consistent volume throughout
- No extremely long gaps of silence
- Minimal mouth clicks and breathing sounds

**Technical Specs:**
- Format: MP3 at 128 kbps or higher (iPhone default is fine)
- Volume level: Between -23dB and -18dB RMS (iPhone handles this automatically)
- Consistent tone and delivery throughout

### Speaking Style
**Critical:** The AI will replicate your performance in the samples.

- If you want energetic YouTube voice → Record with energy
- If you want calm, authoritative tone → Record calmly
- **Keep it consistent** - don't mix monotone and animated in same training set
- Match the tone you want for UnGouge Digest videos

### Content of Recording
- Speak about home improvement/contractor topics (use provided script)
- Conversational, not stiff
- Natural inflections and pacing
- The language in samples should match your content language (English)

## Step-by-Step Recording Guide with iPhone 15 Pro Max

### Equipment You'll Use
- **iPhone 15 Pro Max** (built-in microphone is excellent)
- **Voice Memos app** (pre-installed)
- **Quiet room** (bedroom, closet, or office with door closed)

### Creating a Good Recording Environment

**Option 1: Simple Home Setup**
1. Choose quietest room in house
2. Close windows and doors
3. Turn off AC/heating temporarily
4. Put phone on airplane mode (no notifications)
5. Record in a closet with clothes (they dampen echo)

**Option 2: Improved Setup (Recommended)**
1. Use a small room (not large empty room with echo)
2. Hang blankets or quilts on walls/door to reduce echo
3. Sit at desk with soft materials around (pillows, blankets)
4. Position phone 8-12 inches from your mouth
5. Avoid holding phone (use phone stand or stack books)

**Pro Tip:** Record a 30-second test first. Play it back. If you hear:
- Echo → Add more soft materials (blankets, pillows)
- Background noise → Find quieter location/time
- Too quiet or distorted → Adjust distance from phone

### Recording with Voice Memos App

**Initial Setup:**
1. Open **Voice Memos** app
2. Close all other apps
3. Put phone in **Airplane Mode** (prevents interruptions)
4. Position phone 8-12 inches from your mouth (about a hand's width + a bit more)
5. Use a stand or prop phone up (don't hold it - causes handling noise)

**Recording Sessions:**

**Session 1-6: Record 20-30 minute segments**
1. Tap red record button in Voice Memos
2. Wait 2 seconds before starting to speak
3. Read through the voice sample script naturally
4. Don't rush - speak at your normal YouTube pace
5. If you make a mistake, pause 3 seconds, then continue (you can edit later)
6. Tap Stop when finished

**Between Sessions:**
- Take breaks (avoid vocal fatigue)
- Stay hydrated
- Keep the same recording setup each time
- Aim for same energy level across all sessions

**Naming Convention:**
- VoiceClone_Session1
- VoiceClone_Session2
- etc.

### Exporting Recordings

**For Each Recording:**
1. Tap the recording name
2. Tap the **3 dots** (•••) menu
3. Select **"Share"**
4. Choose **"Save to Files"**
5. Save to iCloud Drive or a new folder called "ElevenLabs_Training"

**Alternative - Send to Computer:**
1. Tap the recording
2. Tap **3 dots** (•••)
3. Select **"Share"**
4. Choose **AirDrop** to your Mac
5. Or email to yourself

## Uploading to ElevenLabs: Step-by-Step

### 1. Create ElevenLabs Account
1. Go to **elevenlabs.io**
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Use email or Google account
4. Verify your email

### 2. Subscribe to Creator Plan
1. Click **Pricing** in top menu
2. Select **Creator** plan ($22/mo)
3. Enter payment information
4. Confirm subscription

### 3. Navigate to Professional Voice Cloning
1. Log in to ElevenLabs dashboard
2. Click **"Voices"** in left sidebar
3. Click **"Add a new voice"** button
4. Select **"Professional Voice Clone"**

### 4. Upload Your Audio Samples

**Option A: Upload Pre-Recorded Files**
1. Click **"Upload samples"**
2. Select your iPhone recordings from Files/iCloud Drive
3. Upload all recordings (you can select multiple)
4. Wait for upload to complete

**Option B: Record Directly (Not Recommended for Full Training)**
1. Click **"Record yourself"**
2. This is better for verification, not for 2+ hours of training
3. Use the upload method for your main training data

### 5. Check Sample Length Feedback
- ElevenLabs will show total audio duration
- **Target: 2-3 hours total**
- Minimum: 30 minutes (but quality won't be as good)
- If under 1 hour, consider recording more

### 6. Process Your Audio (Optional)
1. Click **"Audio settings"** next to each clip
2. Options to:
   - Remove background noise
   - Separate speakers (if multiple - shouldn't be needed)
3. This is automatic enhancement - try it if recordings aren't perfect

### 7. Verify Your Voice
**Important:** This proves you're cloning YOUR voice, not someone else's.

1. ElevenLabs will prompt you to verify
2. **Use the same iPhone and setup** you used for training
3. Read the verification script they provide
4. Speak in similar tone/energy as your training samples
5. If verification fails:
   - Wait 24 hours to try again
   - Or contact ElevenLabs support

**Tip:** Record verification in the same location with same setup to increase success rate.

### 8. Wait for Fine-Tuning
1. After verification, ElevenLabs processes your voice
2. Fine-tuning takes time (can be several hours to a day)
3. Check status in **"My Voices"** section
4. You'll get notification when ready

### 9. Test Your Voice Clone
1. Go to **"Voices"** section
2. Select **"Personal"** tab
3. Click **"Use"** next to your voice clone
4. Type test script (something you'd say in a video)
5. Generate audio and listen
6. Test with different scripts to verify quality

## Tips for Best Results

### Recording Tips
✅ **DO:**
- Record in consistent quiet environment
- Maintain same energy level throughout
- Speak naturally, as you would in videos
- Take breaks to avoid vocal fatigue
- Record multiple shorter sessions vs. one marathon
- Stay well-hydrated

❌ **DON'T:**
- Don't record in echoey rooms
- Don't rush through the script
- Don't mix very different speaking styles
- Don't record when sick or voice is tired
- Don't change microphone/location between sessions
- Don't include background music or other voices

### Content Tips
✅ **DO:**
- Use the provided voice sample script (home improvement focused)
- Speak about topics you'll cover in videos
- Use natural conversational tone
- Include variety of sentence structures
- Show range of emotions (but stay consistent overall)

❌ **DON'T:**
- Don't read in monotone (boring to clone)
- Don't shout or whisper extremely
- Don't include copyrighted content

✅ **DO:**
- Test clone with multiple different scripts
- Compare to your actual voice
- Adjust generation settings if needed
- Save successful generation settings
- Keep original recordings as backup

## Troubleshooting

### "Verification Failed"
**Solutions:**
- Use same device and environment as training recordings
- Match the tone/energy from training samples
- Wait 24 hours and try again
- Contact ElevenLabs support with recording details

### "Clone Sounds Echo-y or Distorted"
**Causes:**
- Training audio had room echo
- Background noise in recordings
**Solution:**
- Record new samples in better environment
- Use audio processing tools to clean before upload
- May need to create new clone with better recordings

### "Clone Doesn't Sound Like Me"
**Possible Issues:**
- Not enough training data (under 1 hour)
- Inconsistent speaking style in training
- Mix of different tones/energies
**Solution:**
- Add more consistent training audio
- Re-record with more consistent delivery
- Ensure all recordings are in same style

### "Running Out of Credits Too Fast"
**Check:**
- Creator plan = 200,000 characters/month
- 1 character ≠ 1 word (character = letter)
- Average 10-min video = ~10,000 characters
**Solution:**
- Monitor usage in dashboard
- Upgrade to Pro ($99/mo = 1M characters) if needed
- Be strategic with generation (don't re-generate unnecessarily)
