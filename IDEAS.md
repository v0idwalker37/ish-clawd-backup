## Investment Bot - Insider Signal Hunter
**Date:** 2026-02-09  
**Status:** Concept / Back burner (focus is Ungouge)

### The Core Idea
CFOs leak earnings in Slack screenshots on Reddit. Product managers mention unannounced features in Discord. Employees gripe about layoffs before the press release. This information exists publicly for a brief window before markets react — and it's worth money.

### What It Does
- **Continuous monitoring agent** scanning Reddit, Twitter/X, Discord leaks, Pastebin, forums, GitHub issues, LinkedIn posts
- **Pattern recognition** for material non-public information (MNPI):
  - Earnings numbers before announcements
  - Layoff plans ("my whole team just got fired")
  - M&A discussions ("we're being acquired")
  - Product launch details
  - Supply chain disruptions
  - Regulatory issues
- **Signal scoring** — separate noise from actionable intelligence
- **Alert generation** — "CompanyX CFO may have leaked Q4 revenue on r/wallstreetbets 47 minutes ago"
- **Trade recommendations** — directional bets with confidence scores

### Why It Could Work
- People are terrible at operational security
- Social media moves faster than compliance departments
- Reddit/Discord have replaced the golf course for accidental insider tips
- AI can scan at scale and connect dots humans miss

### Challenges
1. **Legal gray area** — using public information is legal, but profiting from leaked MNPI is ethically murky
2. **False positives** — 99% of "leaks" are speculation or LARPing
3. **Speed** — by the time you verify, the edge is gone
4. **Market impact** — if everyone does this, the edge disappears

### Possible Structure
- **Scanning tier:** 24/7 monitoring agents (cheap, high volume)
- **Analysis tier:** Pattern matching + credibility scoring (medium cost)
- **Verification tier:** Cross-reference with known insiders, company filings, historical patterns
- **Execution tier:** Auto-trade (if legal) or alert Jason for manual review

### Elevator Pitch
"Most insider trading happens on accident now — someone screenshots the wrong Slack channel, posts earnings to the wrong subreddit, or complains on Twitter using their real name. We build an AI that finds these leaks in real-time, scores their credibility, and generates trade signals before the information goes mainstream. It's not insider trading if it's already public — we're just faster at finding it."

### Next Steps (when ready)
1. Research legal boundaries (consult securities lawyer)
2. Proof of concept: Reddit scraper for one ticker (TSLA, NVDA, etc.)
3. Build credibility scoring model
4. Backtest against historical leaks
5. Paper trading to validate edge
